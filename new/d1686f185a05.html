This 2021 Ram 1500 Big Horn Crew Cab is powered by the 5.7L HEMI V8 with FuelSaver MDS paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, delivering strong HEMI performance with the space and versatility of a Crew Cab.

Equipped with the Big Horn Level 2 Equipment Group, this Ram features heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, remote start, 9-speaker Alpine audio with subwoofer, an 8.4-inch touchscreen, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Park-Sense Front and Rear Park Assist and power-adjustable pedals. It also features the Bed Utility Group with LED bed lighting, adjustable cargo tie-down hooks and a deployable bed step, plus a Trailer Brake Control and anti-spin rear differential.

As part of our reconditioning process, this Ram completed an 85-point inspection and received new front and rear shocks, new front sway bar bushings, new wiper blades and new engine and cabin air filters. Two recall campaigns were also completed. The vehicle is now fully certified.
