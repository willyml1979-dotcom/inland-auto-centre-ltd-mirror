2016 DODGE GRAND CARAVAN

This 2016 Dodge Grand Caravan SE is powered by a 3.6L Pentastar V6 engine paired with an automatic transmission. Finished in Black with a Black interior, this minivan has 174,466 km and offers plenty of interior space, practical features and seating for seven, making it a great option for families or everyday transportation.

This Grand Caravan comes equipped with cloth bucket seats, a 6-inch display, Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, CD player, cruise control, air conditioning, power windows, power locks and fog lights. Additional features include a rearview camera, rear parking assistance, traction control, anti-lock brakes, child seat anchors and a rear window defroster.
