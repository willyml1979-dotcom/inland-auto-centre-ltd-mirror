This 2020 Ram 1500 Rebel Crew Cab is powered by the 5.7L HEMI V8 engine with FuelSaver MDS, paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission. Combining HEMI performance with the Rebel's off-road capability, this truck features an electronic locking rear differential, 3.92 rear axle ratio, Hill Descent Control and a performance-focused suspension.

Equipped with the Black Appearance Package, Leather & Sound Group and Level 2 Equipment Group, this Rebel features leather-faced front bucket seats, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, ventilated front seats, a 12-inch Uconnect 4C NAV touchscreen and a 19-speaker Harman Kardon premium audio system. Additional highlights include remote start, Park-Sense Front and Rear Park Assist, power-adjustable pedals, a spray-in bedliner, trailer brake control and distinctive black exterior accents.

The vehicle was road tested following the completed work and is now fully certified.
